Everything Included

Built for how contractors
actually run their business.

Every feature that matters. Nothing that doesn't.

Calendar View
Month and week views with all upcoming bookings at a glance. Tap any booking for full details and status controls.
Availability Editor
Set weekly recurring hours and block off time-off dates. Customers can only book when you're actually available.
Services & Pricing
Define your services, durations, prices, and buffers. Customers pick the service, you handle the work.
Smart Slot Generation
Automatic slot availability based on your hours, service duration, buffers, and existing bookings — no double-booking possible.
Booking Confirmation Emails
Customers get a branded confirmation email with your logo, their booking details, and all the info they need.
Cloud Sync
Bookings made on the web appear in your app. Calendar edits sync back to the server. Works offline, reconciles when you reconnect.
Custom Domain
Bring your own domain. Point a CNAME and your booking page lives at book.yourbusiness.com — SSL included automatically.
Stripe Billing
Monthly subscription managed through Stripe. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime — from the dashboard, no calls required.
Build Status Dashboard
Track your iOS and Android app builds in real time — Queued → Building → Submitted → Live. No wondering what's happening.
