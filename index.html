Everything Included

Built for how contractors
actually run their business.

Every feature that matters. Nothing that doesn't.

Calendar View
Month and week views with all upcoming bookings. Tap any booking to view details or update status.
Smart Scheduling
Automatic slot generation based on your hours, service durations, buffers, and existing bookings. No double-bookings possible.
Availability Editor
Set your weekly hours and block off time-off dates. Customers only see slots when you're actually free.
Services & Pricing
Define services, durations, prices, and buffer times. Customers pick the service — you do the work.
Confirmation Emails
Customers get a branded confirmation email with your logo, booking details, and everything they need.
Cloud Sync
Web bookings appear in your app. Calendar edits sync to the server. Works offline and reconciles when you reconnect.
Custom Domain
Point a CNAME and your booking page lives at book.yourbusiness.com — SSL provisioned automatically.
Build Status Dashboard
Track your iOS and Android builds in real time — Queued → Building → Submitted → Live. Full visibility, no surprises.
Stripe Billing
Managed monthly subscription through Stripe. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard.
