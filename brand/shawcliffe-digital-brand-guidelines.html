02 — Color System

04 — Logo & Mark

Logo Usage

The Shawcliffe Digital logo consists of a geometric "S" mark and a wordmark. The mark is built from two interlocking angular facets — a mirrored pair that together trace the letter S. The upper shape is Shawcliffe Blue (#114AC4); the lower is Shawcliffe Navy (#040F23). This two-tone construction evokes both digital precision and structural craftsmanship.

05 — Spacing & Layout

The Grid

Layout principle: Prefer two-column or split compositions — as seen on the business card — where a visual anchor (logo or image) occupies the left and contact or content sits right. Use thin horizontal or vertical rule lines (1–2px in Shawcliffe Blue or Light Gray) to divide sections rather than heavy borders or filled bars.

06 — Voice & Tone

How We Sound

Shawcliffe Digital talks like a trusted expert — not a corporate vendor. Our clients are small & independent business owners who value clarity, confidence, and no nonsense. We earn trust through expertise, not jargon.

Confident

We know what we do and say it plainly. We lead with capability and results — no hedging, no over-qualifying.

Practical

We speak in terms of real outcomes for real businesses. Technical terms are explained, never flaunted.

Approachable

We're people, not faceless. Our tone is warm and human — peer-to-peer, never corporate-to-customer.

Precise

Fewer words, more impact. We respect our clients' time and get to the point with clean, structured language.

Tagline: "Branded Lead Capture Systems for Small & Independent Businesses" — this is a positioning statement, not a marketing slogan. Keep it factual and use it in full when introducing the brand.

07 — Do's & Don'ts

Brand Usage Rules

Consistency protects recognition. These rules keep every brand expression — from social posts to proposals — unmistakably Shawcliffe Digital.

✓ Do

  • Use Navy (#040F23) and Blue (#114AC4) as the only primary brand colors
  • Use Montserrat in all-caps for headlines and display text
  • Maintain clear space equal to the mark height on all logo sides
  • Use thin blue or light gray rule lines as dividers
  • Keep layouts clean, structured, and two-tone
  • Use the full wordmark when introducing the brand
  • Keep photography realistic — real businesses, real people

✗ Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo mark in any way
  • Use gradients or drop shadows on the logo
  • Place the logo on busy or photographic backgrounds
  • Use more than two brand colors in a single layout
  • Use decorative, serif, or script fonts anywhere
  • Recolor the logo outside the approved variations above
  • Use neon, bright, or off-brand accent colors
