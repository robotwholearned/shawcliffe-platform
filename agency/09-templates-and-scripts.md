# 9. Templates and Scripts (Copy/Paste)

Fill in `[brackets]`. Keep the tone: friendly, direct, no corporate filler.

## First Outreach Message (cold)

> Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] with Shawcliffe Digital — we build simple websites/apps for local
> businesses like [type]. I noticed [specific observation, e.g. "you don't have a way for people to
> check if you're open"] — is that something worth 10 minutes to talk through? No pressure either way.

## Follow-Up Message

> Hey [Name], just following up on my note from [day] — totally fine if now's not the time, just
> didn't want it to get buried. Happy to chat whenever works.

## Discovery Call Script

1. "Thanks for making time — before I talk about us at all, tell me about your business and how
   things are going right now."
2. "Walk me through what happens when a new customer tries to reach you today."
3. [Use the relevant questionnaire from [04-discovery-templates.md](04-discovery-templates.md)]
4. "Based on what you've told me, here's roughly what I'd recommend — [package]. Does that sound
   like it's addressing the right thing?"
5. "I'll put together a proposal with exact pricing and send it over in the next couple days — sound
   good?"

## Proposal Email

> Subject: Your Shawcliffe Digital Proposal — [Client Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Great talking with you about [specific pain point they mentioned]. Attached is a proposal covering
> what we'd recommend and why — take a look and let me know if anything needs adjusting.
>
> Quick summary: [1-sentence recap of the solution] for [price], with [timeline].
>
> Happy to hop on a quick call if it's easier to talk through than read. Otherwise, just reply "yes"
> and I'll send over the contract and invoice to get started.
>
> [Your Name]

## "Just Checking In" Email

> Hi [Name], no update needed on my end — just wanted to check if the timing's changed on your side
> for [project]. Whenever it makes sense, I'm around.

## Client Onboarding Email

> Subject: Welcome to Shawcliffe Digital — let's get started
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Excited to get moving on [project]! Here's what happens next:
>
> 1. Quick kickoff call — [scheduling link]
> 2. A short homework list (attached) — the sooner we get this, the faster we launch
> 3. We'll keep you posted at every stage
>
> Rough timeline: [X weeks], targeting launch around [date].
>
> Talk soon,
> [Your Name]

## Asset Request Email

> Hi [Name], to get moving we'll need the following from you when you get a chance:
> - Logo file(s)
> - A few photos (phone photos are totally fine)
> - Your hours, contact info, and service/price list
> - Any text/copy you already like, or just answer the attached quick questions if writing from
>   scratch is a hassle
>
> No rush on all of it at once — send what you have and we'll fill in gaps together.

## Revision Request Response

> Thanks for the feedback! Here's what I'm hearing: [bullet summary]. I'll get these updated by
> [date] — let me know if I missed anything.

## Scope Creep Response

> Great idea — that's a bit outside what we scoped originally, so here's what it'd add: [X in time /
> $Y]. Want me to go ahead, or should we save it for after launch?

## Payment Reminder

> Hi [Name], quick reminder that invoice #[number] for [$amount] was due on [date]. Let me know if
> you need anything from me to get it processed — otherwise, just a friendly nudge!

## Launch Announcement

> [Client]'s new [website/app] is live! Check it out at [link] — [one-line benefit to their
> customers, e.g. "now you can see what's fresh before you even leave the house"].

## Monthly Check-In

> Hi [Name], quick monthly check-in from us: everything's running smoothly on our end. Anything
> you'd like changed, added, or fixed this month? Just reply and let us know — otherwise, talk next
> month!

## Support Response

> Got it, thanks for flagging — looking into it now. I'll update you by [time/date] either way,
> even if it's just "still working on it."

## Upsell Suggestion

> Hey [Name], noticed [observation, e.g. "you've been getting a good number of leads through the
> contact form"] — a lot of clients in your spot see even better results adding [next package, e.g.
> automated booking]. Want me to put together quick numbers on what that'd look like?
