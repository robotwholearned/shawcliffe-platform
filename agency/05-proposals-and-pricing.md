# 5. Proposal & Pricing Process

## Proposal Structure (use for every client)

1. **Problem summary** — restate their pain in their own words from discovery
2. **Recommended solution** — the package, in plain language
3. **Features included** — bullet list, specific
4. **What is NOT included** — explicit, prevents scope creep fights later
5. **Timeline** — realistic, with a start-date dependency ("starts once deposit + content received")
6. **Client responsibilities** — what they need to provide and by when
7. **Price** — one-time build + monthly support, shown separately
8. **Monthly support** — what it covers
9. **Optional add-ons** — priced separately, not bundled
10. **Next steps** — literally "reply yes, we send the contract + invoice, work starts on deposit"

Send within 48 hours of the discovery call while the conversation is fresh. See
[09-templates-and-scripts.md](09-templates-and-scripts.md) for the email wrapper.

## Pricing Logic

Treat these as starting anchors, not rigid rules — adjust for real scope, but never quote verbally
without writing it down the same day.

| Package | What it is | One-time price range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter website | 1–5 page site, mobile-responsive, contact info, basic SEO | $800–$1,800 | Most common entry point |
| Website + lead capture | Above + contact/quote form routed to email/SMS, basic tracking | $1,500–$3,000 | Add if they want inbound leads, not just info |
| Custom business app | Owner dashboard + customer-facing app/page (e.g. seller status/inventory) | $3,000–$8,000+ | Scope this carefully — this is where creep happens |
| Notification system | SMS/email/push broadcast to customers (open/closed, back-in-stock, etc.) | $800–$2,000 | Often bundled into the custom app package |
| Owner dashboard | Admin portal to manage hours, prices, status, broadcasts | $1,000–$3,000 | Can be sold standalone or bundled |
| QR code campaign | Physical cards/signs linking to digital profile or ordering | $200–$600 | Cheap add-on, good upsell after a website |

| Recurring plan | What it is | Monthly price range |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly support plan | Hosting, uptime monitoring, minor updates, email support | $50–$150 |
| Maintenance plan | Above + security/platform updates, backups | $100–$250 |
| Higher-touch marketing plan | Above + ongoing content, basic SEO/GBP upkeep, monthly report | $250–$600 |

Full detail on recurring tiers and what's included in each in
[07-client-training-and-support.md](07-client-training-and-support.md).

## Pricing Rules of Thumb

- Never price a custom build without a written scope — verbal quotes turn into fights.
- Quote a range in your head, give a single number in writing. Ranges in a proposal invite haggling
  on the low end.
- Deposit is non-negotiable: 50% to start, balance on launch (or a 3-payment split for larger custom
  builds). No deposit, no work starts — this is the #1 thing that prevents getting burned.
- If a client wants it cheaper, remove scope. Don't just lower the number for the same deliverable.
