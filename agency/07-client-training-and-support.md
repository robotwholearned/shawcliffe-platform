# 7. Client Training, Support & Recurring Revenue

## Client Training Process

Keep this to one short session + a one-page cheat sheet. Cover:

- How to log into their owner dashboard
- How to update products/services
- How to update prices
- How to change open/closed status
- How to see and respond to leads
- How to check notifications sent/received
- How to request support (exact channel — one phone number/email, not three)
- **What not to touch** — clearly mark any settings/technical areas that are ours to manage
- What to do if something looks broken (screenshot it, send it to the support channel, don't try to
  fix it themselves)

Send the cheat sheet as a PDF/one-pager after the live walkthrough — people forget a live demo
within days.

## Support & Maintenance Process

### What's Included (standard support plan)

- Hosting and uptime monitoring
- Security/platform updates
- Bug fixes (something that used to work and now doesn't)
- Small updates (text/photo swaps, price changes if not self-serve, minor tweaks) — cap at a defined
  time budget per month (e.g. 30 min)
- Monthly check-in message

### What's NOT Included

- New features (that's a change request/quote)
- Full redesigns
- Content writing (unless on the higher-touch plan)
- Third-party tool costs (domain renewal, SMS carrier fees, etc. — passed through, not absorbed)

### Response Time Expectations

| Plan | First response |
|---|---|
| Standard support | 1–2 business days |
| Maintenance | 1 business day |
| Higher-touch marketing | Same business day |

Set these expectations in writing at onboarding — "I'll get to it eventually" said out loud becomes
"you never responded" in a client's memory three weeks later.

### Bug Fixes vs. Larger Change Requests

- **Bug** = it used to work, now it doesn't. Fixed under the support plan, no separate quote.
- **Small update** = minor content/config change within the monthly time budget.
- **Larger change request** = new feature, new page, new integration. Quoted separately, same
  proposal-lite process as a new project (problem, solution, price, timeline).

### Monthly Check-In Process

One short message (see template) covering: anything they need changed, anything broken, quick
reminder of what's included in their plan. Doesn't need to be a call unless they ask for one.

### Client Communication Log

Keep a simple running note per client (a doc or a column in your tracker) — every support request,
what was done, when. This is what saves you when a client says "you never told me about that."

### Renewal Process

Support plans auto-renew monthly/annually by default. Send a heads-up before any annual renewal
charge. No surprise charges — ever.

### Cancellation Process

Client can cancel with written notice (define the window — e.g. 30 days). Confirm in writing what
happens to hosting/domain after cancellation (do they keep it, do they need to migrate, is there an
offboarding fee for exported files). Don't hold a site hostage — it damages your reputation more
than the lost revenue is worth.

### Emergency Requests

Define what counts as an emergency (site down, payment broken, notification system silent during
peak hours) vs. what doesn't (a typo, a color they don't like). Emergencies get same-day response
regardless of plan tier; everything else follows normal response times.

## Recurring Revenue System

Recurring revenue is the actual business — the one-time build pays for the work, the monthly plan
pays the bills. Structure it around:

- Hosting/maintenance (the floor — every client should have this)
- Support (response time + small fixes)
- Analytics/reporting (a simple monthly summary, even 3 bullet points)
- SEO basics upkeep (GBP posts, basic keyword checks)
- Notification system fees (SMS carrier costs pass-through + a margin)
- Ongoing improvements (small, incremental — keeps the relationship active)
- Marketing retainers (content, ads management — only for clients who want more than upkeep)
- Content updates (photos, seasonal copy changes)
- Premium support (faster response, more included time)

### Example Monthly Packages

| Package | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $50–$75/mo | Hosting, uptime monitoring, security updates, 15 min small edits |
| Standard | $100–$150/mo | Above + 30 min edits, monthly check-in, basic analytics summary |
| Premium | $250–$400/mo | Above + GBP management, monthly content refresh, priority same-day response |
| Notification add-on | $20–$50/mo | SMS/push sending costs + monitoring, scales with volume |

Sell the recurring plan as part of the original proposal, not as an afterthought — it should feel
like "of course this is included," not a surprise upsell after launch.

### Upsells and Retention

- Check in quarterly even on quiet accounts — ask about their season, not just "need anything?"
- Natural upsell path: website → lead capture → dashboard/notifications → higher-touch marketing.
- Never pitch an upsell during a support complaint — fix the issue first, revisit the upsell later.
