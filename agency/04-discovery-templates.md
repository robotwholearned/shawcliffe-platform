# 4. Client Discovery Templates

Every questionnaire should uncover the same core things — adjust wording per business type:

- How they currently get customers
- Where they lose time
- Where they lose money
- Questions customers ask them over and over
- Manual admin tasks eating their week
- Booking/inquiry problems
- Marketing gaps
- Customer communication problems
- Realistic budget
- Urgency (why now, why not six months ago)
- What would make this genuinely worth it to them

Ask conversationally — this is a guide for you, not a form to read verbatim.

## General Local Business

1. How do most new customers find you today?
2. What do you currently use for a website, if anything?
3. What's the one question customers ask you the most that you wish they could just find the answer
   to themselves?
4. Where do you feel like you're losing time every week on repetitive admin?
5. Have you ever lost a sale because someone couldn't reach you in time?
6. Do you currently collect customer contact info anywhere (email, phone, list)?
7. What's your rough budget range for something like this?
8. Is there a date or event making this urgent (season, anniversary, competitor move)?
9. If this worked perfectly, what would be different in 3 months?

## Service Business (barbers, salons, spas, massage)

1. How do clients currently book with you — call, text, walk-in, an app?
2. How much time per day goes to answering "are you open" or "can I get in today"?
3. Do you ever lose a booking because you didn't see the message in time?
4. Do you track repeat clients or is it all in your head/paper book?
5. How do you currently remind clients about upcoming appointments?
6. What happens when you need to close last-minute (sick day, holiday)? How do people find out?
7. Would clients rebook more if it were easier to see your availability?
8. Budget range and how soon you'd want this live?

## Tradesperson / Contractor

1. When someone needs you urgently (leak, no heat, etc.), how do they find and reach you right now?
2. How many calls a week are "just checking if you're available" vs. actual booked jobs?
3. Do you send quotes/estimates — how, and how long does that usually take?
4. How do you currently track which jobs are quoted, scheduled, done, invoiced?
5. Do you get reviews on Google? Do you ask for them?
6. What's the most annoying piece of paperwork/admin in your week?
7. Would a simple way for people to request a quote online actually get used, or do your customers
   still just call?
8. Budget and timeline?

## Appointment-Based Business

1. What do you use for booking today (paper, phone, an app, nothing)?
2. How often do no-shows happen, and does anything currently remind people beforehand?
3. Can clients see your real-time availability, or do they have to ask?
4. How do you handle cancellations/rescheduling right now?
5. Do you want clients to pay/deposit at booking, or handle payment in person?
6. What would "less back-and-forth scheduling" be worth to you monthly?
7. Budget and urgency?

## Food Seller / Roadside Seller / Pop-Up Seller

1. How do customers currently know when and where you're set up?
2. Do you sell out of certain items — how do customers find out you're out of stock?
3. How do you let people know you're open today vs. closed (weather, supply, etc.)?
4. Do you have regulars who'd want a text/notification when you're set up nearby?
5. What's the most annoying part of managing "what's left" during a selling day?
6. Do you take pre-orders currently, or is it all in-person?
7. Budget and how soon (is there a season this needs to be ready for)?

## Small Church / Community Organization

1. How do members and visitors currently find event times, service times, or updates?
2. What's the most common thing people call/email/ask about that could just be posted somewhere?
3. Do you send out announcements — how (bulletin, phone tree, Facebook, nothing formal)?
4. Is there a way for someone new to find and reach you online right now?
5. Do you collect any contact info for visitors or newcomers?
6. Who currently updates your website/socials, and how much of a burden is that on them?
7. Budget — is this coming from a ministry/operations budget, and who approves it?
8. Any urgency (anniversary, building project, new pastor/leader wanting a refresh)?

---

**Must-have now:** General + whichever 1–2 verticals match your actual pipeline.
**Nice-to-have later:** The rest, filled in as you land clients in those categories.
