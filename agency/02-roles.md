# 2. Core Internal Roles

Two people, nine functions. At the start, most of these collapse into two buckets: **client-facing /
business** and **build / technical**. Split by strength, not by gender-default — pick whoever is
actually better at talking to strangers vs. whoever is better heads-down.

| Function | Practical responsibility | Owner at start |
|---|---|---|
| Sales / client relationships | Respond to leads, run discovery calls, send proposals, follow up | Partner A (people person) |
| Strategy | Decide what services to offer, pricing, positioning | Both — but one is tiebreaker |
| Project management | Keep projects moving, own the checklist, chase content/assets from clients | Partner A |
| Design | Layout, brand look, UI decisions | Whoever has the eye for it |
| Development | Actually building the site/app/dashboard | Partner B (technical) |
| QA / testing | Click through everything before it goes live | Whoever didn't build it (fresh eyes catch more) |
| Client support | Answer support requests, triage bugs vs. change requests | Partner A first line, Partner B for anything technical |
| Billing / admin | Invoices, contracts, renewal tracking | Partner A |
| Research & development | Evaluate new tools, build reusable templates/components | Partner B, done in slow weeks |

## Which Roles Merge at the Start

- **Sales + Project Management + Support** are one job early on: whoever talks to the client owns
  them end to end. Don't hand a client off between people if you can avoid it — it feels like a
  corporate agency and slows everything down.
- **Design + Development** are one job if only one of you builds. Don't hire this out until you have
  a design backlog that's actually blocking launches.
- **Billing/Admin** is a once-a-week batch task, not a role. Do it Friday morning, don't let it live
  in your head all week.
- **R&D** only happens when nothing is on fire. It's the first thing to drop when you're busy — that's
  fine and expected.

The one rule: QA should never be done by the same person who built the thing, even if that means
your spouse just clicks through it for 15 minutes. Fresh eyes catch the broken form.
