## Discovery call questions (~40–45 min, once she responds)

**Business basics**
- Is it just you baking, or do you have help?
- What's the core lineup — GF sourdough, treats — and what sells best?
- Do you sell only direct-to-customer, or also wholesale/cafes/markets?

**Customer flow**
- Right now, does someone find you and then DM, call, or show up in person?
- When a batch is ready, how do people find out — do you post on Instagram each time?
- Do you track who ordered what, or is it all in your head/DMs?

**Time waste**
- What's the question you answer over and over in DMs? (availability, delivery, cross-contam safety, etc.)
- Do you ever lose track of an order or forget to reply?
- How much time a week goes into messaging back and forth vs. actual baking?

**Missed money**
- Does bread sell out before everyone who wants it gets some?
- Do people ask for things you don't have a clear way to request (custom orders, standing weekly order, pickup times)?
- Any regulars you wish you could notify the moment a fresh batch drops?

**Current tools**
- Confirm: Instagram + placeholder website + phone, anything else?
- Google Business Profile?
- Anything tracking orders today (notebook, notes app, spreadsheet)?

**Ideal outcome**
- If this worked perfectly, what's different in 30–60 days?
- What do you want to stop doing manually?
- What's a "yes, worth it" outcome for you?

**Budget/timeline** — gauge fit, don't over-probe on a first call.

**Component clues to listen for:** "I sell out" → Product Inventory/Limited Batch. "People message everywhere" → Lead Capture. "I forget who ordered" → CRM Lite/Notifications. "I do custom orders" → Photo Upload + Inquiry Form.
